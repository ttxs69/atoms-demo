Understanding what you can create with Atoms, its capabilities, and project boundaries will help you get the most out of your AI agent team.

## What You Can Build with Atoms

With Atoms, you can build a wide variety of web-based projects:

- **Personal Websites**: Full websites, landing pages, or portfolios with AI-assisted design, typography, and layout.
- **Web Apps**: Interactive tools and mini-applications without writing complex code, including analytics dashboards, calculators, and interactive widgets.
- **Deep Research & Reports**: Advanced research reports, academic summaries, and market analyses powered by our Deep Research feature (Iris) on Pro or Max plans.

You can also explore ideas and remix projects created by other creators in **App World**.

## Complex Commercial Software

Atoms is optimized for rapid prototyping, data visualization, web applications, and custom mini-games. We are continuously enhancing our agent architecture to support increasingly complex software systems over time.

## Mobile & Web App Capabilities

Currently, Atoms focuses primarily on **Web application development**. While native iOS/Android binary apps and desktop executables are not directly generated, you can deliver app-like mobile experiences using:

1. **Progressive Web Apps (PWAs)**: Web apps that can be installed directly onto mobile home screens, providing offline support, push notifications, and a near-native mobile experience.
2. **Responsive Web Applications**: Built with modern stacks like TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Shadcn-ui for smooth rendering across all screen sizes.
