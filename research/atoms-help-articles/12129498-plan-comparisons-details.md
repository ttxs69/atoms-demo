Compare Atoms subscription plans, features, annual discounts, and credit rollover policies to find the right tier for your needs.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Atoms Annual Payment Plan">
### Go Annual, Get More

To provide users with more flexibility and cost savings, Atoms offers an annual payment option for Pro and Max plans.

### Annual Plan Discount

- **Discount**: 21% discount (first tier) or 18% discount (non-first tier) compared to 12 consecutive monthly payments.
- **Savings & Credit Flexibility**:
  - Enjoy 25 bonus credits like Free and Monthly planners.
  - Annual credits are allocated in 12 equal monthly installments.
  - Unused credits each month automatically roll over within the same billing year (up to your annual limit).
  - When renewing an annual plan, unused credits from the previous annual cycle can roll over to the new cycle (up to the annual credit limit).

### Plan Switching & Rollover

- **Upgrade (Monthly → Annual)**: Takes effect immediately. Unused monthly credits roll over with the annual plan for up to 12 months.
- **Downgrade (Annual → Monthly/Free)**: Takes effect at the end of the current annual cycle. Current annual benefits remain active until the cycle ends.

### Policy Notes

- Annual plans are non-refundable once the billing period begins.
- Invoices and billing details can be viewed on your **Plans and Billing** page.

  <Accordion title="Max Package">

### Target Users & Overview

Designed for high-demand power users, teams, and developers requiring maximum computing resources, disk space, and advanced modes.

### Features & Specs

- **Price**: Starting from $100 / month
- **Daily Allowance**: 15 free credits allocated daily at 00:00 (America/Los_Angeles) (Up to 25 per month)
- **Monthly Credits**: Flexible options ranging from 500 up to 10,000 monthly credits (500 / 1000 / 1500 / 2500 / 3750 / 5000 / 6000 / 7500 / 10000)
- **Disk Space**: 100 GB disk space
- **Compute Power**: 2x computing resources versus Pro
- **Exclusive Capabilities**:
  - Race Mode (higher accuracy and execution speed)
  - Private projects
  - Data download
  - Atoms badge removal
  - Direct code editing

### Target Users & Overview

Perfect for first-time or occasional users exploring Atoms capabilities at zero cost.

### Features & Specs

- **Price**: Free ($0 / month)
- **Credits**: 15 daily allowance (up to 25 credits per monthly cycle)

<Accordion title="What’s the difference between Free, Pro, and Max?">

### Overview Comparison

- **Free Plan ($0 / month)**: 15 credits/day (up to 25/month), 2GB storage, forever free for light exploration.
- **Pro Plan (From $20 / month)**: 100–350 monthly credits + daily allowance, 10GB storage, private projects, badge removal, and code editing.
- **Max Plan (From $100 / month)**: 500–10,000 monthly credits + daily allowance, 40GB storage, 2x compute resources, and Race Mode for maximum power and speed.

  <Accordion title="Can I switch plans? When will the change take effect?">
  Yes, you can switch anytime.

Upgrade It takes effect immediately after successful payment, but the billing cycle will not be reset, and it will still be calculated according to the original cycle. Upgrading the package only requires paying the price difference.

Example If you are on a $20 plan with 20 days left in the cycle, you can upgrade to the $100 plan by paying the $80 difference. The upgrade is effective right away and covers the remaining 20 days.

Unused credits will roll over to the next month, but can only be carried over once and will not accumulate indefinitely.

Downgrade It will take effect on the first day after the end of the current billing cycle. If the storage space is reduced, the system will remind you in advance to export or clean up projects to prevent any impact on the normal use of MGX. Downgrading a package does not result in a refund, and users must wait until the end of the billing cycle.

Example If you purchased a $100 plan and used it for 10 days (with 20 days left in the cycle), and then downgrade to a $20 plan, the downgrade will not take effect immediately. You need to wait until the $100 plan expires at the end of the cycle, after which the $20 plan starts the next day.

Unused credits will roll over to the next month, but can only be carried over once and will not accumulate indefinitely.
