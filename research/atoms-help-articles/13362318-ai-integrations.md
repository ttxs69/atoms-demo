Imagine building your own team of AI agents—a personal fitness coach, a private tutor, or even a creative partner for generating scripts and manga.

Whether you need productivity tools or an AI companion for emotional support, the AI revolution is here, and you shouldn't be left behind.

We offer seamless integration with the world's most advanced LLMs, including **Gemini 3.0 Pro, ChatGPT 5.1, and Claude Sonnet 4.5**, empowering you to build the future today.

## How to Enable LLM Integration

To connect your agent with advanced Large Language Models, follow these steps:

1.  **Toggle on Atoms Cloud** on the homepage.
    
2.  **Describe Your Needs:** Mention in your prompt that you wish to use an LLM (or describe a task that requires one).
    
3.  **Automatic Setup:** The Atoms agent will automatically recognize this requirement and integrate LLMs for you.
    

## When to Use AI Integration

### 1\. Creative Content Generation

If your agent needs to produce original text, stories, or scripts from scratch, it requires an LLM.

**Examples:** A manga script generator, a poetry writer, a marketing copy assistant, or a brainstorming partner for plot twists.

### 2\. AI Customer Support

Standard bots can only answer exact keyword matches. By integrating an LLM via **Atoms Cloud**, your Customer Support Agent can understand context, handle spelling errors, interpret the user's intent, and respond with a polite, professional tone.

To set this up:

1.  **Enable Integration:** Activate the LLM connection via Atoms Cloud.
    
2.  **Add Knowledge:** Supply the agent with your specific business details or documents. This ensures the AI doesn't just chat generally, but answers customer inquiries using _your_ company's accurate information.
    

### 3\. AI Tutors

Education is no longer one-size-fits-all. By building an AI Tutor with Atoms, you can create a private instructor that adapts entirely to your learning style. Unlike a static textbook, an AI Tutor integrated with an LLM can break down complex concepts into simple analogies, generate practice quizzes on the fly, and patiently re-explain difficult topics as many times as needed. Whether you are mastering a new language, studying for a history exam, or learning to code, simply upload your study materials and define your goals.

These use cases are simply starting points to demonstrate the power of AI integration. You are not limited to these categories. With the flexibility of **Atoms Cloud**, you can customize your agent’s personality and knowledge base to solve problems we haven't even listed.
