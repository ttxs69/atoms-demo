## Joining the community

You can join or follow Atoms community and social channels from the Atoms dashboard.

From the Atoms dashboard Home page, click **Join our Community** in the left sidebar. The modal shows the currently available community tasks and credit rewards.

Current community tasks may include:

**Discord：**

Discord is a community and support channel.Join the Atoms Discord community to talk with other users, ask questions, share ideas, report issues, and discuss Atoms in real time.

**X (Twitter)：**

X is a social update channel. Follow Atoms on X for product announcements, feature updates, launch news, tips, and short-form updates from the team.

**LinkedIn：**

LinkedIn is a social and company news channel. Follow Atoms on LinkedIn for company updates, product news, industry insights, and professional announcements.

**Reddit:**

Reddit is a community discussion channel. Join the Atoms Reddit community to share projects, discuss use cases, ask community questions, and exchange tips with other users.

Completing available tasks can earn up to **25 credits**. The available tasks, reward amounts, and supported channels may change, so the in-product **Join our Community** modal is the source of truth.

Other Atoms channels may also include **GitHub** as a developer community channel for exploring Atoms repositories, examples, and developer-related updates. This is different from the GitHub connector in Settings > Connectors, which is used to connect GitHub to Atoms, and it does not count as a Join our Community credit task unless GitHub appears in the in-product modal.

**Note:** Community and social channels are different from Atoms connectors. Only services shown in Settings > Connectors can be connected as Atoms connectors.

## Getting help with the Atoms AI Bot

To enhance the self-service experience, the Atoms documentation site now features an integrated **AI Bot Assistant**. If you experience any issues with Atoms, you can quickly search the docs, browse FAQs, or chat directly with the AI Agents for personalized support through the **[Help Center](https://help.atoms.dev/en)**.

### How to use it:

- Click the AI Bot chat bubble at the bottom of the page to start a conversation.
- Type your question in the input field.
- Press Enter or click Send to submit.
- The AI will respond within 1–2 seconds, sometimes with helpful documentation links.
- You can keep asking follow-up questions—multi-turn conversations are supported.

## Contacting the support team

If you have any further questions, please don't hesitate to contact us at [service@atoms.dev](mailto:service@atoms.dev).
