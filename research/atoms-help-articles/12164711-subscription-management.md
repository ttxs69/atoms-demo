# Subscription Management

- Source: https://help.atoms.dev/en/articles/12164711-subscription-management
- Summary: Guide to subscription auto-renewals, plan upgrades, downgrades, and data storage limits
- Updated: Jul 29, 2026

---

Learn how to manage your subscription, switch plans, understand auto-renewal policies, and handle data storage during upgrades or downgrades.

## Auto-Renewal Policy

**Both Pro and Max plans renew automatically on a monthly basis**, unless you actively downgrade before the start of your next billing cycle.

You can view your current plan, remaining credits, and billing history anytime via **Settings → Plans and Billing** or the **Pricing → Payment** page.

## Canceling or Managing Your Subscription

You can cancel your paid subscription anytime by downgrading to the **Free plan**. The downgrade will take effect on the first day after your current billing cycle ends.

### 2 Ways to Manage Your Plan

1. **Via Account Settings**:
   - Open the main menu on the left sidebar.
   - Click the **Settings** icon.
   - Select the **Plans and Billing** tab to view your current subscription, update payment methods, or change your plan.
2. **Via the Pricing Page**:
   - Click **Pricing** in the top-left navigation bar.
   - Manage, upgrade, or downgrade your active plan on the Payment page.

## Changing Plans (Upgrades & Downgrades)

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time:

### Upgrading Your Plan
- **Immediate Effect**: Upgrades take effect immediately after successful payment.
- **Billing Schedule**: Your billing cycle date is not reset; it continues on the original schedule.
- **Prorated Cost**: You only pay the price difference for the remainder of the billing period.
  - *Example*: If you are on a $20 plan with 20 days left and upgrade to a $100 plan, you only pay the $80 difference to receive immediate access to $100 plan features.

### Downgrading Your Plan
- **End-of-Cycle Effect**: Downgrades take effect after your current billing cycle ends. You maintain your current plan benefits until the end of the term.
- **Refund Policy**: No refunds are issued for downgrades.
- **Credit Rollover**: Unused credits roll over to the next month once (they do not accumulate indefinitely).

## Impact on Your Data & Disk Storage

If downgrading or upgrading alters your available disk storage limit (e.g., from 40GB on Max to 10GB on Pro or 2GB on Free), the system will send you a reminder to back up or delete project files. This ensures your project files remain safe and prevents any interruption to your daily work.
