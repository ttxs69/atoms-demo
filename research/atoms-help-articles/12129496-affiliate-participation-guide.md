## What is an Affiliate Program?

An affiliate program allows you to earn commission by referring others to a product or service. You share a unique link, and if someone signs up and pays through it, you earn a reward.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="How does the Atoms Affiliate Program work?">
Our affiliate program is designed to reward both you and the people you invite. You'll get a unique referral link. When someone signs up and makes a payment using your link, the following rewards apply:

**Promotional Period (October 2025 – March 2026)**:

- **For you**: Earn a **30% commission** on each referred customer's first six payments.
- **For your friend**: Receive a bonus of **10 credits** for signing up and sending at least one message.

**Standard Rewards (Effective after promotional period)**:

- **For you**: Earn a **22% commission** on each referred customer's first six payments.
- **For your friend**: Receive a **10 credits bonus**.

*Note: Invitations prior to the launch of the Affiliate Program are not included.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who can join the affiliate program?">
Anyone with an audience, including creators, developers, bloggers, influencers, or product users, is welcome to apply.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I refer users if I’m not an Atoms customer?">
**Absolutely!** You do not need to be an active Atoms user to join our affiliate program. It is open to everyone. While not required, we do find that familiarity with Atoms helps create more authentic and effective referrals.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a limit on how many people I can invite?">
**No.** You can invite as many people as you like. The more users you bring in, the more recurring commission you can earn.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do invited users get any benefits?">
**Yes.** Each invited user will receive **10 Bonus Credits** after successfully registering via your referral link and sending at least one message in the chat box.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
