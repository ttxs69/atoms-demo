## What is the Issue Report?

When AI Agents run into issues during building, an Issue Report notification appears in the bottom-left corner. You can click "Resolve" to fix it instantly, or expand the report to view error logs and details on the right panel.

Don't panic. Knowing what to do upfront saves you time and credits.

## What You Need to Know Before You Start

When an error occurs, Atoms displays an Issue Report notification in the bottom-left corner of the screen.

**The notification shows:**

* A brief description of the error
* A "Resolve" button to fix all issues automatically
* An option to expand the report for detailed error logs

The right panel displays the Bug Report with detailed error logs, stack traces, and system information to help you understand what went wrong.

**You can choose to**:

* Click "Resolve" and let the AI fix everything automatically
* Or expand the report to review the technical details before deciding

## How to Report an Issue

### Scenario 1: The page is stuck and no "Resolve" button appears

If your page is frozen or unresponsive, and you don't see the Issue Report notification:

* Open the chat with the AI Agent.
* Click the "..." (more options) next to their latest message.
* Select "Feedback" and describe the issue with your Chat Link and a screenshot.

This sends the report directly to our support team. We'll reach out to help you.

### Scenario 2: You see the "Resolve" button

Follow the steps below to resolve the issue.

**Step 1: Click "Resolve"**

Simply click the "Resolve" button. The AI agent will automatically analyze and resolve all identified bugs.

Usually takes 1 to 3 minutes.

Important: Wait for each attempt to finish before clicking again. Multiple clicks create duplicate tasks and waste time.

**Step 2: Check the result**

Once the AI finishes, your preview will refresh. If it doesn't update, try refreshing your browser first. Sometimes it's just a cache issue.

**Step 3: If the issue persists**

If the error still appears after clicking "Resolve", you can manually check the error logs in the right panel for more details. If you're unsure how to fix it, contact support directly.

* Open the chat with the AI Agent.
* Click the "..." (more options) next to their latest message.
* Select "Feedback" and provide:
  * Your Chat Link
  * A screenshot of the error or issue
* Our support team will investigate and help you directly.
