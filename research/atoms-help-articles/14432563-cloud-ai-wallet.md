# Cloud & AI Wallet

- Source: https://help.atoms.dev/en/articles/14432563-cloud-ai-wallet
- Summary: The Cloud & AI Wallet is where you pay for your app’s Cloud and AI usage.
- Updated: Jul 12, 2026

---

## **What is the Cloud & AI Wallet?**

The Cloud & AI Wallet is used to manage balances for Cloud (compute, storage, network) and AI (model usage, agents, etc.).

There are three types of balances:

-   **Cloud & AI (Wallet Balance):** Prepaid amount to pay for the usage of Cloud and AI.
    
-   **Cloud:** Your remaining free quota for Cloud usage.
    
-   **AI:** Your remaining free quota for AI usage.
    

## **How do I top up the Cloud & AI Wallet?**

1.  Click **Top Up** on the Cloud & AI page.
    
2.  Select an amount (preset options or custom input between $10 and $1,000).
    
3.  Choose a payment method (credit/debit card, Amazon Pay, Alipay, etc.) and confirm.
    
4.  Once payment succeeds, your balance updates immediately. If it fails, try another payment method.
    

## **When is the AI Wallet used?**

The AI Wallet is used in both preview and production for AI capabilities in your applications (e.g., text, image, and video generation). Once your **free AI quota ($1)** is exhausted, you will need to top up the Cloud & AI Wallet to continue using these capabilities.

## **How do I set balance alerts and monthly limits?**

Access in the top-right corner of the Cloud & AI page.

-   **Balance Alerts:**
    
    Receive email or in-app notifications when your balance drops below a set threshold. You can enable it with a default threshold (typically $5) or customize it.
    
-   **Monthly Limit:**
    
    Set a cap on total monthly spending from your wallet (e.g., default $500).
    
    This limit applies to combined Cloud & AI usage.
    
    For example, with a $100 limit, Cloud $40 + AI $60 will trigger it.
    
    When the limit is reached, you can choose:
    
    -   **Pause all apps (default):** Stops all Cloud and AI-related applications.
        
    -   **Notify only:** Sends alerts without pausing services.
        
    
    If you set a new limit below your current usage, it will take effect immediately and may pause your apps.
    

**Note:**

Actual monthly charges may slightly exceed the set limit because:

-   Usage is billed after consumption.
    
-   Cloud storage is billed hourly, so already accrued costs may still be charged after suspension. This is expected due to:
    
    -   **Postpaid billing:** Usage is charged after consumption, so already incurred usage will still be billed.
        
    -   **Hourly storage billing:** Storage costs are charged hourly; usage generated before suspension will still be billed.
        
    -   **Trigger timing:** The limit is enforced only after reaching or exceeding the threshold, so totals may slightly exceed it.
        
-   The monthly limit is designed to control risk and prevent unlimited spending, not to enforce a hard stop.
    

## **How can I view Cloud and AI usage?**

Go to the **Usage** tab in the Cloud & AI Wallet:

-   View **AI Usage** and **Cloud Usage** summaries
    
    (Cloud is further broken down into Compute, Storage, Network, and Other).
    
-   See usage **by project**, including:
    
    -   AI usage
        
    -   Cloud usage
        
    -   Open Chat entry for quick access to adjust configurations or traffic
        

Usage data is updated daily (UTC 0:00), helping you identify high-cost projects and optimize accordingly.

## **What can I see in Transactions?**

The **Transactions** tab shows money flow only (not detailed usage charges).

Types include:

-   Manual Top-up
    
-   Auto Top-up
    
-   Credit granted (platform credits)
    
-   Refunds/adjustments
    

Click any record to view details for reconciliation or confirmation.

## **What payment methods are supported?**

You can add and manage payment methods in **Manage Billing**, Top Up, or Auto Top-up flows, including:

-   Credit/debit cards
    
-   Amazon Pay
    
-   Alipay
    

Once added, they are available for both manual and automatic top-ups.

## **Can Free users top up the wallet?**

No. Free users must upgrade to Pro or Max before they can top up.

The Cloud & AI Wallet page will guide you to upgrade. After upgrading, top-up, auto top-up, and payment management become available immediately.

## **Why did my application suddenly stop?**

Most likely, both your wallet balance and free quotas have been used up.

Check your balance in the Cloud & AI Wallet. Please enable Auto top-up or Balance Alerts to avoid service interruptions.

## **Are charges for Cloud or AI? How do I identify the project?**

In the **Usage** tab, you can view usage by project and distinguish between Cloud and AI costs. The **Transactions** tab only shows financial movements (top-ups, refunds, credits), not detailed usage charges.
