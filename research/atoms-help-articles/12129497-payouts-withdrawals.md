Comprehensive guide on affiliate commission payout schedules, supported payment methods, and withdrawal limits for the Atoms Affiliate Program.

## Payout Schedule & Payment Methods

Affiliate commissions are calculated and paid out on a **monthly schedule** via **Wise**.

To ensure seamless monthly transfers:
1. Log into your affiliate dashboard after registering.
2. Provide and verify your registered **Wise email address**.
3. Ensure your payment payout details are kept up to date before the end of each billing cycle.

---

## Withdrawal Limits & Minimum Thresholds

**No.** There is **no earning cap** and **no minimum withdrawal threshold**.

- **No Minimum Balance**: Even small commission amounts earned during a billing period will be processed and transferred via Wise during the next scheduled payout cycle.
- **No Maximum Cap**: There is no limit to how much total commission you can accumulate and withdraw.
