Before you begin your journey with Atoms, review the prerequisite requirements and tips to make your development process seamless.

## Programming Knowledge Requirements

**No coding or software development experience is required.**

Simply describe what you want to build using natural language in the chat prompt. Your AI teammates will handle requirement analysis, system architecture, front-end development, back-end integration, and deployment automatically.

### Tips for Better Results

- **Be specific with your goals**: Describe the layout, key features, and visual style you want.
- **Provide reference material**: Upload images, wireframes, or documents to guide the agents.
- **Give iterative feedback**: If an output isn't quite right, chat with your agents to refine the result.
- **Technical users**: If you have technical experience, you can specify frameworks, libraries, or API structures to guide the implementation.
