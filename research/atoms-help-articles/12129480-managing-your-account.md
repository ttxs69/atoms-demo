## How can I delete my account

We’re sorry to hear that you’d like to leave Atoms. Please review the process and important notes below before proceeding.

## Steps to Delete Your Account

1. Please get in touch with our support team directly through the chat box on this page. You should submit the following statement to begin the process:

   **I confirm the deletion of my account: \[Registered Email\]**

   This step is required to verify authenticity and prevent accidental deletion.
2. Our technical team will process your request. Once completed, you’ll receive a confirmation email.

## Important Notices

1. **Permanent action**: Deleting your account will permanently erase all data, including subscriptions and chat history. We cannot reverse the process.
2. **Subscriptions**: Active subscriptions will continue billing until cancellation. Please cancel any auto-renewals in advance.
3. **Data handling**: All operations follow strict security and compliance procedures to ensure your information is properly managed.

We value your time with us and hope to welcome you back in the future.
