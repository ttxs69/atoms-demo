# Team Workspace

- Source: https://help.atoms.dev/en/articles/12129353-team-workspace
- Summary: Invite your teammates to your workspace, share credits, and build projects together seamlessly.
- Updated: Jul 29, 2026

---

Atoms allows you to collaborate with your team in real time. By upgrading to the Pro or Max Plan, you can bring your designers, developers, and product managers into a single unified space to build together faster.

## What is a Workspace?

A workspace is your team's shared environment for organizing projects and managing billing in one place. Every user gets a default workspace upon sign-up. By subscribing to the Pro or Max Plan, you can invite unlimited team members to collaborate.

You can easily switch between workspaces by clicking your workspace avatar and name in the top-left corner of the sidebar.

## Inviting Teammates

You can add and manage collaborators from the People tab. Simply go to Settings > People.

1. Go to the "Invite workspace members" section.
2. Enter the email addresses of the teammates you wish to invite (you can paste multiple emails at once).
3. Click "Invite".

Invited members will receive an email with a link to join. In your People list, their status will show as "Invite sent" until they accept, after which it will update to "Active".

**Note:** All members in a workspace share the credits from the workspace's centralized billing plan. You can track how many credits each member has consumed under the "Total Usage" column in the People list.

## Building Together

Our chat interface supports synchronous, real-time collaboration. All users in a workspace can enter the same Chat page simultaneously and interact with the Agent.

You will be able to see all historical messages sent by other users, distinguished by their avatars and names.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="How are credits billed?">
We use centralized billing. All members invited to your workspace utilize the shared credit pool of your Pro or Max Plan.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How many members can a workspace have?">
Pro and Max plans support unlimited members. Free plan workspaces are limited to the Owner only (1 seat).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I belong to multiple workspaces?">
Yes. You can join as many workspaces as you're invited to. Switch between them using the sidebar.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Who pays for the workspace?">
The workspace Owner's plan determines the credit pool. All members share the same credits. Members use credits from the workspace they're currently in, not their personal workspace.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
