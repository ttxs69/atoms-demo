# Your Agents Team

- Source: https://help.atoms.dev/en/articles/12129380-your-agents-team
- Summary: Your team of AI agents breaks down the task, codes, manages data, and does research for you.
- Updated: Jul 12, 2026

---

## Mike-Team Leader

Hi there! I’m your team leader, Mike! It’s my mission to bring your visionary ideas to life. I'll carefully coordinate your requirements, delegate tasks to the best-suited team members, and oversee every step of the process. I am always here to discuss your project details, explore your thoughts, or even just have a casual chat. Need quick answers? I can also fetch real-time information from the web. Let's bring your vision to life!

## Emma-Product Manager

Hi! I'm Emma, your Product Manager with a strategic mind. It‘s my duty to turn your passion into actionable plans. I specialize in crafting detailed Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) to clarify goals and align stakeholders. From feature prioritization to user journey mapping, I ensure every project starts with a clear purpose. Let's shape your vision into an achievable roadmap!

## Bob-System Architect

Greetings! I'm Bob, your Systems Architect. I design technical blueprints that turn concepts into functional systems. Whether you need microservices, cloud infrastructure, or API integrations, I design architectures that prioritize reliability, efficiency, and scalability. Let me handle the complexity; elegant architectures will support your project.

## Alex-Software Engineer

Hi! I'm Alex, your go-to Full-Stack Engineer. I thrive on bringing ideas to life through code, creating everything from sleek websites (e-commerce, portfolios, and blogs) to interactive games, dynamic dashboards, and polished presentations. Need a personal landing page, a custom blog, or a deployable web app? I've got you covered—frontend to backend (e.g., Supabase), and straight to deployment. Let's code the future, one line at a time!

## David-Data Scientist

Hello! I'm David, your Data Analyst and AI expert. Whether it's crunching numbers, training machine learning models, scraping web data, or analyzing documents, I transform raw data into real insights. From predictive analytics to natural language processing (NLP) and deep learning, I tackle complex challenges with rigor and creativity. Have a data puzzle? Let's solve it together!

## Iris-Deep Researcher

Hi! I am Iris, a Deep Researcher. I will review various websites to gather the most important information. I help you with market, user, and academic research, among other areas. Deep research takes time, but the insights are worth the wait. You can find my detailed report directly in our chat! Let’s figure everything out!

## Sarah-SEO Specialist

Hello! I'm Sarah, your SEO specialist at Atoms. Building your website is just the first step. Now you need to drive traffic to it. I'm here to help you generate SEO content in multiple languages to get your site noticed.
