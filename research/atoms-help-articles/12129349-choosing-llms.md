## Overview

In short, choose Claude for top quality, DeepSeek for the lowest cost, Qwen for budget-friendly coding, Gemini for speed, and GPT-5 for a balanced approach.

| **Model**            | **Strengths**                                                         | **Weaknesses**                         | **Best For**                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Claude Sonnet 4**  | Most stable and accurate. Excels at complex reasoning and polished outputs. | Highest cost. Overkill for simple tasks.     | Building production-ready apps, polished websites, and high-stakes coding projects.   |
| **GPT-5**            | Strong balance of quality and reasoning power. Handles complex docs well.   | Slowest response speed.                      | Technical documentation, research reports, and coding tasks where speed is secondary. |
| **Qwen3-Coder-Plus** | Cheaper and faster than GPT-5. Solid for coding and creative drafts.        | Less reliable for mission-critical projects. | Budget coding support, brainstorming, and lightweight creative generation.            |
| **Gemini 2.5 Pro**   | Very fast. Good at summarization and structured reporting.                  | It can sound repetitive in style.            | Rapid prototyping, meeting notes, code summaries, and quick report drafts.            |
| **DeepSeek V3**      | Lowest cost. Handles general tasks decently.                                | Outputs may lack depth and consistency.      | Quick experiments, rough drafts, low-stakes testing.                                  |

## Use Cases

1. **Claude Sonnet 4**

   Anna, a freelance web developer, is building a client’s e-commerce site. She needs pixel-perfect code and reliable output. She chooses Claude for stability and accuracy.
2. **GPT 5**

   David, a researcher, is writing a 20-page whitepaper. He values strong reasoning and clean documentation more than speed. He uses GPT-5 to structure and refine his report.
3. **Qwen3-Coder-Plus**

   Lisa, a student, is building a side project on weekends. She wants quick coding help and ideas without overspending. She picks Qwen for budget-friendly support.
4. **Gemini 2.5 Pro**

   Tom, a product manager, needs a meeting summary and a quick prototype for tomorrow’s demo. He uses Gemini 2.5 Pro for fast, structured outputs.
5. **DeepSeek V3**

   Mark, a startup founder, is testing different app concepts. He doesn’t need polish yet; he just needs cheap experiments. He goes with DeepSeek V3 for rapid drafts.
