## Cloud & AI Billing Guide

Cloud & AI usage is charged based on the actual resources consumed by your published applications.

* **Cloud Infrastructure**: Compute, database storage, serverless executions, and object storage.
* **AI Model Inference**: API usage for GPT, Claude, and LLM calls in published apps.
* **Quota Priority**: Free Cloud/AI quota is used first. Additional usage is deducted from your Cloud & AI Wallet.
* **Resource Pricing**: Database storage (\$0.525/GB-month), Serverless requests (\$0.30/1M requests), Object storage space (\$0.025/GB-month).
* **Monitoring**: Check usage daily under **Settings** → **Cloud & AI** → **Usage**. Set balance alerts or monthly limits to control spending.

## How can I get my refund?

In principle, once the service begins (i.e., when credits start being consumed), payments are non-refundable. However, refunds or credit compensations may be granted under specific qualifying conditions.

**How to Request a Refund**:

1. Click your avatar → **Help Center** → click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner to contact support.
2. Provide your registered account email, invoice screenshot, and proof of payment.
3. Specify whether you are requesting a cash refund or credit compensation. Our support team will review and respond within 24 hours.
