# Credit Management

- Source: https://help.atoms.dev/en/articles/12129499-credit-management
- Summary: Guide to credit usage, daily limits, subscription tiers, rollover policies, and balance tracking
- Updated: Jul 29, 2026

---

Learn how credits work, daily allowances, subscription allocations, credit rollover policies, and balance tracking in Atoms.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can I get compensation for credits?">
We understand that unexpected issues may occur during the building process.
​
If your issue remains unresolved or you need further assistance, please contact our support team and include:
​
- A clear description of the problem
- Relevant screenshots
​
In some cases, we may provide compensation in the form of **credits** (which cannot be exchanged for cash).
​
**Compensation will NOT be provided for:**
​
- AI output quality issues, unsatisfactory results, or problems caused by custom prompts/user settings.
- User environment issues, scheduled platform maintenance, rate limits, or third-party service disruptions.
​
Multiple compensation requests related to the same issue will be reviewed and processed together.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I buy extra credits if I run out?">
Yes. You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time on the **Settings → Plans and Billing** page or the **Pricing → Payment** page to receive additional credits immediately.
​
You only pay the prorated price difference when upgrading your monthly plan.
​
- **Pro Users**: Choose from three credit tiers: 100, 250, or 350 credits per month.
- **Max Users**: Choose from multiple credit tiers ranging from 500 up to 10,000 credits per month based on your workload.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Will my unused credits be reset to zero?">
Whether unused credits expire depends on your plan and credit type:
​
### Credit Rollover Rules
​
Unused credits will automatically roll over to the next billing cycle for **one month only** (they do not accumulate indefinitely) in the following cases:
​
1. Unused credits generated when a monthly subscriber upgrades mid-cycle.
2. Bonus credits granted under special circumstances (excluding the daily 15-credit gift).
3. Referral credits earned from inviting new users.
4. Credits redeemed via a Redemption Code.
​
*Note: All rolled-over credits expire after one additional month.*
​
### Annual Subscriptions
​
For annual plans, credits remain available according to annual rollover rules throughout your 12-month billing period.
​
### Subscription Cancellation
​
If you cancel a paid subscription, all unused and rolled-over credits will expire at the end of the current billing cycle.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="When do free daily credits reset?">
Free daily credits reset every day at **00:00 (America/Los_Angeles)**.
​
- **Daily Allowance**: 15 credits per day.
- **Monthly Cap**: Up to 25 free daily credits in total per monthly plan cycle.
​
Once you hit the monthly limit of 25 free credits, daily refreshes pause until the start of your next billing cycle.
​
*Notice: Upgrading to Pro or Max does not reset your billing cycle date. New plan credits will be issued according to your schedule.*
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How can I check my credit balance or remaining balance?">
You can check your remaining credit balance anytime under **Settings**:
​
- **Dark Purple Section**: Displays remaining active credits in your account.
- **Gray Section**: Displays consumed credits.
- **Usage Metrics**: Exact numerical usage breakdown is displayed directly beside the progress bar.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What are credits, and how are they used?">
Credits are the universal currency used to redeem AI agent services across Atoms.
​
### 3 Ways to Earn Credits
​
1. **Daily Gifts**: 15 free daily credits (up to 25 per month).
2. **Monthly/Annual Subscriptions**: Allocated tier credits.
3. **Bonus Points**: Referral rewards, promotional codes, and support compensation.
​
### System Deduction Order
​
When performing actions, Atoms automatically deducts credits in the following priority:
​
1. Daily Free Credits
2. Subscription Credits
3. Bonus Credits
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why didn’t I receive credits even though I paid?">
In most cases, this occurs because the transaction did not successfully go through. Even if you initiated the recharge, the payment may have **failed silently**. Common reasons include:
​
- The payment was **declined by your issuing bank**.
- The transaction **timed out** before completion.
​
### How to Confirm Payment Status
​
1. **Stripe Checkout Page**: Check the final status of your transaction.
2. **Email Inbox**: Check whether you received a notification regarding a failed transaction.
3. **Settings → Plans and Billing → Manage Billing**: Review your active subscription and transaction history.
​
*If you see a failed payment, please contact your issuing bank. Once the payment is successfully processed, your credits will be added automatically.*
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why didn’t my free daily credits refresh today?">
Your daily credits didn’t refresh because you’ve reached your monthly cap. Each account receives up to 25 free credits per month. While there is a daily allocation of 7.5 credits, the monthly total cannot exceed 25. After you reach 25 in a month, daily refreshes pause until your next monthly cycle begins.
</Accordion>
