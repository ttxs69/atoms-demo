# Affiliate Marketing Policies

- Summary: Comprehensive guidelines on promotional rules, paid ads restrictions, performance analytics, and cookie tracking
- Updated: Jul 29, 2026

Comprehensive guidelines on promotional rules, paid advertising restrictions, link performance analytics, and referral cookie tracking duration for the Atoms Affiliate Program.

## Can I Use Paid Advertising to Promote My Affiliate Link?

**No.** To maintain a fair and clear promotional environment for everyone, Atoms strictly prohibits the use of affiliate links in any paid advertising campaigns.

This prohibition includes, but is not limited to:
- **Search Engine Ads**: Google Ads, Bing Ads, Yahoo Search Ads.
- **Social Media Ads**: Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads.
- **Paid Display & Native Networks**: Any channel requiring payment to display your referral link.

*Policy Enforcement*: This rule prevents brand confusion and avoids direct competition between affiliates and official Atoms marketing. Any violation will result in immediate termination of your affiliate account and total forfeiture of all accrued and future commissions.

---

## Tracking Link Performance & Analytics

**Yes.** After registering for the affiliate program, you will gain access to a real-time affiliate dashboard.

Through your dashboard, you can track:
- **Click-through Counts**: Real-time traffic generated by your referral links.
- **User Conversions**: Successful sign-ups and active paid subscriptions.
- **Earned Commissions**: Detailed breakdown of accrued referral payouts.

---

## Allowed Promotional Channels & Ethical Standards

**Yes**, you can promote Atoms across your website, social media, blogs, newsletters, and content channels, provided your promotions are **ethical and legal**.

- **Permitted**: Original reviews, tutorial videos, blog posts, newsletter mentions, social posts.
- **Prohibited**: Unsolicited email spam, brand keyword bidding (bidding on "Atoms" or related trademarks), and false or misleading product claims.

---

## Referral Tracking Window & Attribution Duration

Your affiliate referral link is **permanently valid**, but tracking relies on a **30-day cookie window**.

1. **Attribution Window**: A referred user must sign up for an Atoms account within **30 days** of clicking your referral link for the attribution to lock to your account.
2. **Commission Duration**: Once a user registers within the 30-day window, their **first 6 payments** will be credited to you as commission.
3. **No Payment Expiration**: There is no time limit on when those 6 payments occur. You can share your link once, and continue earning commissions whenever the user pays, even months later.
