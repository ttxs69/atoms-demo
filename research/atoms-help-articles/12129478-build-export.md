## Can Atoms export apps as .apk files directly?

Atoms doesn’t support generating .apk files directly. It currently works with web apps.


## Does Atoms support building and deploying Next.js projects?

Atoms supports generating and editing full Next.js project code, including pages, components, and APIs. You can also download the generated files for local development or manual deployment. However, Atoms currently does **not support deploying Next.js applications directly o**n the platform.

To publish your Next.js project, you can download the project from Atoms and deploy it manually.
